Tips

  • Plug the FM-1 in with a USB cable, turn it on, then press Connect MIDI (USB) and allow the permission.
  • On FM1 Open, track N listens on MIDI channel N (track 8 on channel 10). Set each track’s channel here to match the FM-1 track that holds that sound. Channels are remembered.
  • Sound # sends a Program Change. It switches voices on DX7 tracks; 303 and 808 tracks ignore it.
  • Song: every change is saved to the selected sequence automatically. + New adds a blank sequence, Copy makes a duplicate you can tweak, and the arrows move it. Play song plays the sequences in order, then starts over. Click a sequence while it plays to jump to it at the next bar.
  • Click or drag across squares to paint steps. Space bar plays and stops. Arrow keys move around the grid and Enter toggles a step.

Bluetooth (experimental)

Bluetooth needs Chrome’s Web Bluetooth. Don’t pair the synth in the computer’s Bluetooth settings first, or it may show up as a speaker instead.
